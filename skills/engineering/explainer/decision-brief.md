# Decision brief

A decision brief puts calls in front of the user so they can rule on each one responsibly. It may stand alone, or sit as a page in another artifact, such as a spec whose open points have piled up. It is usually one page; a call whose grounding runs long gets a page of its own, linked from its card.

## Reasoning

1. **Calls.** List every item from the effort's open points, sorted as the `sort-findings` skill says. Done when each is a call, or sits in sort-findings' fix, repair, noted, or dismissed bucket with what happens to it.
2. **Tangles.** Find which calls depend on which. Done when each group's upstream call is named.
3. **Scale.** Mark each call small, meaning cheap and easy to undo either way, or not. Done when each small call has its one line: the call, the pick, and why.
4. **Weigh.** Weigh each call that isn't small as the `weighing` skill says (Goal, Today, Buys, Better option, Cost, Recurring cost, Caution, Tangles, Pick). When the stakes are small or the cost is unmeasured, one option is to observe and alert, and revisit when it fires. Done when every field is filled; the user most often reshapes a pick whose reasoning skipped one.

**The takeaway carries** the calls the user rules on, upstream first, each with its pick, what gets measured after the ruling, and what result would reverse it.

## On the page

- Tangled calls come first, grouped, with the upstream call on top.
- Each call that isn't small is a decision card (Decision cards below); the small calls follow, one line each.
- The items that turned out not to be calls sit in one short list.

## Decision cards

A decision card holds one call. Its blocks: a Heading 3 with the question; a paragraph giving its **Goal** and how it works **Today**; a table with one row per option and columns for what it **Buys**, what changes and for whom, its **Cost**, and its consequence for the product's users; and the **Pick** in one line in a 💡 callout, with what gets measured after the ruling and what result would reverse it.

## Agent context

Light. Under each card: the sources for its numbers, the files or records each option touches, and the measurement that would reverse the pick.
