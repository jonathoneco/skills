# Notes without a header

Files written before the agent-notes skill keep their names, unless they sit in the wrong place, and `INDEX.md` may not list their folder yet. The first time a session works in such a folder, it:

- adds the header to every note in that folder;
- moves a loose file at the root into its effort's folder, or if it is a machine fact, into the repo that owns that machine's config, as the core rules say, and its `.bak` copies into `vN/`, updating every path that pointed at it as SKILL.md's Keeping notes findable says;
- gives the folder its `INDEX.md` line.

**Old marks.** Entries in older notes end with `→ <destination>`, `→ open`, or `→ declined <date>`. They need no upkeep.
